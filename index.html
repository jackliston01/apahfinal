The Two Axes

The Life Purpose Compass attempts to quantify an individual's on human purpose.

Doomer/Optimist (x-axis): Do you believe that humanity will perservere and prosper well into the future? Doomer is another woord for pessimist.

Logic/Emotion (y-axis): Is human logic sufficient to think about purpose or must emotion be involved to handle what is to complex for reason.

Hopefully, when these axes are combined, they paint a relatively good picture of an individual's stance. Importantly, hough optimism may not be directly related to someone's beliefe of life purpose, I included it in the hopes of adding more nuance. For example, an existentalism might reject both reason and emotion, but will likely be more "doomer."

Methodology

After clicking "Begin Quiz," you will be asked 6 relatively loaded multiple choice questions that are meant to gauge your stance,

In an effort to curb bias, both questions and answer choices are randomized. Once all the questions are answered, the website calculates a value for each of axis. These values (out of necessity) are relative.

This quiz was inspired by the political compass quiz.
